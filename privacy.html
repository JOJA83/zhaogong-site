Prelaunch notice · 29 October 2026

Privacy

This provisional notice covers the informational prelaunch website and enquiries. It is not a final privacy policy for a live recruitment platform.

Who operates this website

ZhaoGong is operated by Zhaomu Pty Ltd. Privacy enquiries can be sent to admin@zhaogong.com.au.

Information on this website

This website has no account registration, application forms, payment collection or phone-number collection. Its pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers or site-set cookies.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request information to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

If you email us

Your email may include your name, email address and any details you choose to provide. That information is used to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence. Please do not send identity documents, financial details or other sensitive information.

Email and hosting providers may process information to deliver their services, including outside Australia. Provider locations and retention arrangements for a future platform have not yet been finalised. This notice does not promise Australian-only storage or a fixed retention period. Contact us about the handling or deletion of your enquiry.

Future accounts and SMS OTPs

If introduced, SMS OTPs are intended to verify control of a mobile number for registration or sign-in and help protect accounts, not send marketing. This website does not currently send SMS. Before activation, updated notices will explain the information collected, purposes, providers, disclosures, overseas processing, security and retention arrangements.

Access, correction and concerns

You can email us to ask about personal information supplied in an enquiry, request access or correction, request deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to confirm your identity before responding. Deletion may be subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may also seek guidance from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Updates

This notice will be reviewed as the service develops. The date above identifies this version. Review the published policy before using any future account or recruitment features.
